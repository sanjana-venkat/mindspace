**Ask from anywhere.**

There is now one Ask button, at the bottom of every screen: the ring, the list, inside a topic, inside a note. Click it, or press ⌘F, and it opens into a question box. Type, press Return, and the answer comes back with its sources, as before. Matching notes and topics still show up as you type, if you would rather jump straight to something.

Inside a note's write-up the same button asks about that note, and the answer appears right under it.

Your questions are now kept in a private file on your Mac, readable only by you. Nothing in the app shows them yet; they are the start of what the recall dial will learn from. You can delete the file at any time: `~/Library/Application Support/Notefy/questions.jsonl`.

Update with:

    brew update && brew upgrade --cask mindspace

Or download the DMG below and drag Mindspace to Applications.
