**A short tour for your first launch, and easier reading.**

- **First-run tips.** Six short tips point at the moon, the ring, Ask, the ring/list switch, New topic and the moon on your desktop, one at a time. Return or Next moves on; Esc skips. Settings → Show tips brings them back.
- **Easier to read.** The open question box and its matches sit on a near-solid frost with darker text, so a busy page behind no longer bleeds through. Captures you are not on are no longer faded to grey.
- **The ✕ puts the question box away**, shrinking it back into the Ask button.
- **Ask is easier to see in light mode.**
- **Zoom in the captures grid** steps by an even 15% and shows a clean number. ⌘+, ⌘− and ⌘0 work too.

Update with:

    brew update && brew upgrade --cask mindspace
