**Your folders are now a ring around the moon, and you can ask them questions.**

Open Mindspace and the library is a mind rather than a filing cabinet. Each folder is an arc, and the length of the arc is that folder's share of everything you have saved, so you can see where your attention has actually gone. Click an arc to open the folder. The grid view is still there, one toggle away in the top right.

**Ask your own notes.** There is a question box under the moon. Ask it something and the answer comes back built only from what you saved, with the captures it used attached as chips you can click to land on the exact screenshot or voice note it came from. An answer you cannot check is just a confident stranger talking.

**Tap the moon and talk to it.** The ring turns into a level meter, your words appear as you speak, and Send asks the question. Nothing is recorded to disk: asking is not saving.

**Every summary can be interrogated.** Open a note's write-up and there is an "Ask this note" box under the sources. Answers cite the same numbered sources listed above them.

**A fix worth knowing about.** macOS stops you launching the same app twice but says nothing about two different copies, so a build left running for a week could quietly put its week-old view of your folders back. Mindspace now takes a lock on your notes folder. A second copy tells you which one already has it and touches nothing. Your notes were never at risk from this, but folder organization was.

Update with:

    brew update && brew upgrade --cask mindspace

Or download the DMG below and drag Mindspace to Applications.
