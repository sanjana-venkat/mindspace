**Topics fan out from the ring, the moon has a face, and a lot of fixes from testers.**

**Open a topic from the ring** and its notes fan out on a curve beside the moon. Five at a time; scroll or use the arrow keys to go through them, Return to open one, click anywhere else to put them away. New note works while they are open.

**A list view** sits next to the ring for when you have many topics, with the search to filter them.

**The moon reacts.** Tap it and it listens with a smile and blinks as you talk, then looks up when you stop. Return sends. It only says "Saved" once you have kept a capture, and puts the folder down after a while.

**Captures, full size.** Double-click any capture to see it on a dark background, and swipe or use the arrows to go through the rest. The grid keeps every tile the same size, with wide screenshots across two columns.

**Highlight text the way you would expect.** Pick Highlight text from the moon, then select what you want. It waits until you have finished selecting.

**Ask a note.** The question box under a write-up stays at the bottom of the window, and answers scroll into view.

**Fixes testers asked for**
- Setup no longer reappears. If a capture needs a permission that is off, Mindspace asks for just that one.
- Clicking anywhere on a folder opens it.
- Long model lists in setup scroll.
- Setup now explains what Mindspace is for and what using it looks like.
- New libraries start with Learning, Reading and Ungrouped. "New folder" is now "New topic".
- Light mode colours on the ring.

Update with:

    brew update && brew upgrade --cask mindspace

Or download the DMG below and drag Mindspace to Applications.
