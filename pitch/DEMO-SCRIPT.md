# Mindspace demo, narration script

For `Screen Recording 2026-09-27 at 2.10.33 PM.mov`, 1:58 long.
259 words, about 132 words a minute. That is a calm pace with room to breathe,
not a race. Captions in `demo-captions.srt`.

The story the footage already tells: the Mac app catches your day, Gemini writes
it up, and the twin is the thing you talk to. The best part is accidental. You
are building the pitch and the prototype on camera, and capturing both as you go,
so the demo quietly proves the product on itself.

## Reading it

Land on "capture it" at 0:46 so the card appears on the word. The last line wants
a real pause before it, about a second, and no lift at the end. It is the title
of the thing, not a question.

Two words to resist: "just" and "basically". Neither is in the script and both
tend to appear when nerves do.
