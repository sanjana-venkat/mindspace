## 1 · Hook

> OPEN HUMAN
>
> # Mindspace
>
> ## Imagine you could Cmd + F your brain.
>
> A personal digital twin, grounded in what you capture, think and learn.

**Visual.** The moon, centred right, ringed by twelve folder arcs sized by share.

> THE PROBLEM
>
> # We collect everything. We return to almost none of it.
>
> SCREENSHOTS · VOICE NOTES · DOCUMENTS · HALF FORMED THOUGHTS
>
> *What did I save?*
> *Why did it matter?*
> *How does it connect to what I am thinking about now?*
>
> The gap is not storage. It is return.

**Visual.** The same arcs, broken into scattered unjoined fragments across three

**Speaker notes (38).** We screenshot, we take notes, we record voice memos, then it
sits there. The gap is not storage, it is return. What did I save, why did it matter,
how does it connect to what I am working on now?

## 3 · The belief

> THE BELIEF
>
> # Use AI to preserve human intelligence.
>
> Storing what we read is easy. Keeping the thinking around it is not. Your questions,
> your objections and your interpretations deserve to be preserved beside the things
> you saved.
>
> A TWIN YOU CAN: brainstorm with · debate · learn from · reflect against
>
> A digital twin here is an evolving reflection of the knowledge and thoughts you
> choose to share. It is not a copy of you.

**Visual.** One large ring bleeding off the right edge, dimmed. No moon.

**Speaker notes (35).** Open Human exists to preserve human intelligence. Not only
what you read, but what you thought about it. Your twin is an evolving reflection of
the knowledge you choose to share. Something to think with.

## 5 · The experience  (hero slide)

> **MOBILE PROTOTYPE CONCEPT**
>
> # Then, talk to what you know.

Three phones:

1. **Home.** Moon centred, folder arcs around it, "tap the moon and talk", an ask bar
   at the foot. Caption: *Home. Folders as arcs, voice at the centre.*
2. **Folder sheet.** The home dimmed behind, a bottom sheet with the folder name, its
   count, and its topics. Caption: *Tap an arc for its topics.*
3. **Topic.** Serif AI summary, source chips underneath it, your own note in italic,
   two suggested follow ups, a chat bar. Caption: *Tap a topic for summary, sources,
   chat.*

**Speaker notes (43).** This is what we are prototyping. Folders become arcs around
the moon, sized by how much of your mind lives in each. Tap the moon and talk. Tap an
arc for topics. Tap a topic for a summary, your notes, and a chat bar.

## 6 · One use case

> **ILLUSTRATIVE SCENARIO**
>
> # From recall to thinking.
>
> **You.** What was that idea I saved about making recordings feel more natural?
>
> **RETRIEVED FROM YOUR NOTES · 14 SEP** *"Recording feels like a performance. What if
> it just listened, and showed the words as they came?"*
>
> **Mindspace.** You wanted the ceremony gone. No start button, a live transcript, the
> recorder out of the way.
>
> **You.** Challenge that. What am I missing?
>
> **Mindspace.** Always listening raises a trust question, and a live transcript
> invites editing while you speak. Your note assumed both were free.

**Legend.** Teal is retrieved from your own saved material. Lavender is Mindspace
interpretation, grounded in it. Marked as an illustrative scenario, not a recorded
result.

**Speaker notes (38).** Here is the shift. First you ask for something you half
remember, and it comes back with the original note attached, so you can see where the
answer came from. Then you ask it to argue with you.
