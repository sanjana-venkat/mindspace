## M1 · One memory of you  *(built on V1's layout)*

**OPEN HUMAN · THE MEMORY LAYER**

# One memory of you.

Our long-term vision is the perfect user memory system: one that any AI agent or
LLM product can connect to, to offer the best personalization possible.

> Notes: Alongside the recall dial, this is where the company goes. Every AI
> product wants to personalize. Personalization is only as good as what it knows
> about the person, and we want to build the memory every AI plugs into to know you.

## M2 · How AI knows you today  *(V2's three cards)*

**HOW AI KNOWS YOU TODAY**

# AI learns about you in three ways.

| 01 Interactions | 02 Agent learning | 03 Connectors |
|---|---|---|
| **What you tell it.** Your conversations with ChatGPT or any assistant, whenever you open it for something. | **What it learns.** The agent's own learning from its successes and failures. | **What your tools hold.** Gmail, Workspace, calendars and files: your digital world, through connectors. |

*Only one of these comes from your world.*

## M3 · Need-based  *(V3's two findings)*

**NEED-BASED DATA**

# Two of the three are about the AI, not you.

Interactions and the agent's learning exist only because the AI has become part
of your life. They're about the system and how it should perform, and they arrive
on a need basis.

- **Need-based — Interactions and learning.** Gathered when you open the AI
  because you need something. Shaped by the task, not by your day.
- **Natural — Connector data.** The only one of the three that comes naturally
  from your world.

*What's missing is the part of your world you never hand over.*

## M4 · The fourth kind  *(V7's ruled list)*

**THE FOURTH KIND**

# Your own mind.

Your own thoughts, your journals, the things you read and learn. It's a natural
part of your world, and today's AI systems don't capture it.

- **Not impossible.** It could be captured. Nobody has made it easy.
- **Friction.** You'd have to think things through, brainstorm on purpose, or
  upload notes with no task in mind.
- **No reason.** Most people never find a need that makes it worth doing.

## M5 · Where Mindspace comes in  *(V2's three cards) — to build*

**WHERE MINDSPACE COMES IN**

# Capture the fourth kind, naturally.

| 01 Journal | 02 Capture | 03 One place |
|---|---|---|
| **Think it down.** Journal and take notes as you go, without a task in mind. | **Keep what you meet.** Meetings, screenshots, things you read, each with your thought. | **All of your day.** Everything kept together, not spread across apps. |

*We don't only learn about you when you talk to us. We learn about you every day.*

> Notes: The more people see Mindspace as their journal, the more we know about
> what they experience and everything they're exposed to. That doesn't come from
> someone having a specific need and talking to an agent. It comes from them
> documenting what they go through.

## M6 · Why people will use it  *(V6's three cards) — to build*

**WHY PEOPLE WILL USE IT**

# Utility first: learning on top of your memory.

| 01 Retrieve | 02 Rekindle | 03 Remember your way |
|---|---|---|
| Search and ask across everything you've kept: your digital footprint. | Bring back what you learned and started to forget. | Choose how much help you want remembering, with the recall dial. |

> Notes: People won't journal just because we ask. The product has to be a
> better experience and more useful than anything else they use. So we start with
> AI-driven learning: store your notes and journals and whatever you capture about
> your day, and retrieve over it — to augment your thoughts and memory, rekindle
> your learning, and remember things the way you want to.

## M7 · How it adds up  *(V9's now/next/later cards) — to build*

**HOW IT ADDS UP**

# Four kinds of data. We hold the missing one.

| Connectors | Interactions | Your mind |
|---|---|---|
| **Already open.** Available through APIs to anyone who builds. | **Come with the product.** Once people use Mindspace, we have these too. | **Only we capture it.** Journals, thoughts and learning, kept because they're useful to you. |

*Proactive assistants share the same memory underneath. We take the same route
from a different need — journaling — so the memory starts out richer.*

> Notes: Muse got proactiveness, and uses connector data to act on it, but the
> underlying memory layer is the same. That's why OpenAI could launch Dot: same
> memory, organized in a single thread. We're going a similar route, but through
> a different user need, journaling and documenting everyday things, so we have a
> better picture of the user. We won't learn about the user only when they talk to
> us. We learn about them every day.
