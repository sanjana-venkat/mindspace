Mindspace pitch deck · Figma plugin
Open Human

BUILD THE DECK

  1. Open your Figma file (Slides or Design, either works).
  2. Menu bar: Plugins > Development > Import plugin from manifest...
  3. Choose manifest.json from this folder.
  4. Plugins > Development > Mindspace Decks, then pick
     Pitch deck, or Long-term vision

Ten slides appear.

  1  Hook                    5  The experience
  2  The problem             6  One use case
  3  The belief              7  Team and close
  4  The foundation

  A1 Market and model        A3 Next twelve months
  A2 Where we sit

The three A slides are the appendix. They are not part of the two minutes.
They exist so that when someone asks about market, competitors or the plan,
you flip to a prepared answer instead of improvising one.

BEFORE YOU PRESENT

  · Every number on A1 and A3 is a dashed blank. Fill them in yourself.
    Enrolment figures are public. Do not say a number you have not checked.
  · Slide 7 has a dashed placeholder for the demo link or QR. Replace it.
  · In Figma Slides, set the deck size to 1920 x 1080 first. The plugin will
    stop and tell you if it is anything else.

IF IT FAILS

  A window opens naming the step that failed and the error. Send that text.

Speaker notes are attached to each slide in Figma Slides, and sit under each
frame in a Design file.
